HOW TO USE

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.0 RELEASE NOTES
  • Auto Volume Leveling: Balances loud and quiet local songs automatically without modifying your audio files, with a one-tap Analyze Library option.
  • Headphone Crossfeed: Choose Off, Subtle, or Room for a more natural, less fatiguing headphone soundstage.
  • Gear Profiles: Switch between Pure Flat, Earbuds, Car / Speaker, Late Night, or your own custom sound profiles in one tap.
  • Three-band EQ & Favorites clear: Fine-tune Bass, Mid, and Treble or clear all Favorites safely in one confirmed action.
YOUR MUSICLIBRARY
  • Add music: Open Playlist, tap Add Music, and choose your audio files. Imported songs are saved in this app on this device.
  • Make a playlist: Tap Add Playlist, enter a name, then choose it from the playlist list.
  • Favorites: Tap the heart on the record to add or remove the selected song. You can clear all Favorites safely without deleting songs.
  • Remove songs carefully: Deleting a song from All Tracks permanently deletes its stored audio from this app. Removing a song from a custom playlist or Favorites only removes it from that list.
PLAYBACKCONTROLS
  • Play and skip: Tap Play or Pause to control music. Tap Previous or Next to change songs.
  • Rewind and fast-forward: Press and hold Previous or Next.
  • Song position: Drag the progress bar to move to another part of the song.
  • Play mode: Tap the play-mode button to switch between Normal, Repeat One, and Shuffle.
  • Sound & EQ: Tap the top-left sound icon for Gear Profiles, Auto Volume Leveling, Headphone Crossfeed, and Bass, Mid, and Treble controls. Reset returns everything to Pure Flat.
  • Lock screen: You can also use the Android lock-screen and notification controls.
BLUETOOTH AND RESTARTPLAYBACK
  • Bluetooth: If your headphones disconnect, playback pauses. Reconnecting does not restart music. Press Play when you are ready.
  • Open the app again: Your last selected song and saved position are restored. Playback stays paused until you press Play.
SLEEP TIMERTIMER
  • Set a timer: Tap the clock at the top, choose a saved time, or tap the number to enter minutes. Then tap Start.
  • Manage the timer: Tap Stop to cancel or Reset to clear the selected time. You can add and edit saved timer presets.
  • When time is up: The player pauses when the countdown finishes. For the most reliable countdown, keep the app open.
Keep your music safe. Your imported songs are stored inside this app on this device. Keep backup copies of your original audio files. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove your imported music.
